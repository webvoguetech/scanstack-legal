Privacy Policy

Paperkeep · Last updated 23 September 2026

The short version. Paperkeep scans and reads your documents entirely on your phone. Nothing is uploaded unless you sign in and explicitly ask for a backup — and if you do, the backup is encrypted on your device first, with a password only you know. We cannot read your documents.

Who we are

Paperkeep is operated by WebVogue. For anything in this policy, contact contact@webvogue.in.

What happens on your device

Scanning, edge detection, image enhancement, text recognition (OCR) and document categorisation all run locally on your phone. The text extracted from your documents, the page images, and the categories and titles derived from them are stored only in Paperkeep's private storage on your device.

Paperkeep does not request camera permission. Capture happens inside Google Play Services' own scanner, which hands Paperkeep only the finished pages.

What we collect

If you never sign in: nothing. There is no account, no analytics identifier and no document upload.

If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive and store:

  • Your Google account identifier and email address, used solely to work out which backup belongs to you.
  • A single encrypted backup file, if and when you ask for one.

How backups are protected

Before anything is uploaded, your documents are packaged and encrypted on your device using AES-256-GCM, with a key derived from a password you choose. That password is never transmitted, never stored, and is not recoverable by us or by Google.

The consequence is deliberate and worth stating plainly: if you forget your backup password, your backup cannot be recovered by anyone, including us.

Who your data is shared with

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Two service providers are involved only where you have opted in:

  • Google Play Services — provides the on-device scanner and text-recognition models. Document content is processed locally.
  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Storage) — stores your account identifier and, if you use cloud backup, your encrypted backup file.

How long we keep it

Your encrypted backup is retained until you replace it, delete it, or delete your account. Deleting your account removes both your backup file and your authentication record. Documents held only on your phone are removed when you delete them or uninstall the app.

Your choices and rights

  • Use the app without an account. Every scanning feature works signed out.
  • Delete your account and backup at any time from Menu (☰) → Profile → Delete account in the app. This is immediate and permanent.
  • Export your data using Back up documents, which writes an encrypted archive to a location you choose.
  • Access, correction or complaint — contact us at the address above.

Depending on where you live you may have rights under the GDPR or India's DPDP Act, including access, correction, erasure and portability. The in-app controls above are designed to let you exercise these directly.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: open Menu (☰) → Profile → Delete account. This deletes your encrypted cloud backup first and then your authentication record. It is immediate and permanent, and cannot be undone.

If you have already uninstalled the app: email contact@webvogue.in from the address you signed in with and ask us to delete your account. We will confirm within 30 days. Full steps are on the delete your account page.

What is deleted: your encrypted backup file and your account record, both permanently. What is not deleted: documents stored only on your own phone — those never reached us, and you remove them by deleting them in the app or uninstalling it.

Children

Paperkeep is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date above and note the change in the app's release notes.
