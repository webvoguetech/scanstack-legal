Paperkeep

An offline document scanner. Everything stays on your phone.

Point your camera at a receipt, invoice, ID or contract. Paperkeep finds the edges, straightens the page, reads the text, and files it by type — all on your device. No account needed, nothing uploaded.

On-device OCR

Text is extracted locally, so your documents never need to leave the phone to become searchable.

Files itself

Receipts, invoices, IDs, contracts and notes are recognised automatically. You can always correct it.

Expiry alerts

Spots expiry and due dates on IDs, policies and invoices, and flags the ones running out.

Encrypted backup

Optional backups are encrypted on your device with a password only you know, then stored wherever you choose.

Why no account?

Because scanning does not need one. Sign-in exists only if you want a cloud copy of your encrypted backup — and even then, the file is encrypted before it is uploaded, so it cannot be read by us or by anyone storing it.
