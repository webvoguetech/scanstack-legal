Delete your Paperkeep account

Paperkeep: Doc Scanner · operated by WebVogue · Last updated 23 September 2026

An account is optional in Paperkeep. It exists only to hold an encrypted backup of your documents. Deleting it removes that backup and your sign-in record permanently. Documents stored on your phone are not affected.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Paperkeep.
  2. Tap the menu (☰) in the top-left corner.
  3. Tap Profile at the top of the menu.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This deletes your encrypted cloud backup first and then your account. It happens immediately and cannot be undone. You may be asked to confirm your Google sign-in again before the deletion goes through.

Delete it without the app

If you have uninstalled Paperkeep or no longer have the phone, email contact@webvogue.in from the Google address you signed in with, with the subject Delete my Paperkeep account. We will delete your account and backup and confirm by email within 30 days. We only act on requests sent from the signed-in address, so that nobody else can delete your account.

What is deleted, and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Encrypted cloud backup fileDeleted permanently, immediately
Account record (email address, Google account ID)Deleted permanently, immediately
Documents, page images and text on your phoneNot touched. They never reached us. Remove them in the app (Settings → Delete all documents) or by uninstalling.
Crash reportsContain no account or document data. Deleted automatically by our crash reporting provider after 90 days.

We keep nothing else after deletion. There is no retention period for account data: once it is deleted it is gone, including from our side.
